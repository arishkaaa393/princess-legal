Якщо у вас є майбутні оплачені записи, радимо спершу скасувати їх у застосунку, щоб отримати повернення коштів.

Account deletion

"Princess Dance Studio" app · FOP Daryna Khripkova

Option 1: in the app (instant)

Open the app → Settings tab → Delete account → confirm. The account and personal data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: without the app

Email arishkaaa393@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and the phone number your account is registered to. We may send a verification code to that number. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: name, phone number, profile photo, notification tokens, your reviews, current bookings.
  • Anonymized (no longer linked to you): completed payment records — kept solely for the Studio's financial accounting.

If you have upcoming paid bookings, cancel them in the app first to receive a refund.
