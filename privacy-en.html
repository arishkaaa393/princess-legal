Privacy Policy

"Princess Dance Studio" mobile application · Effective August 28, 2026

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Individual Entrepreneur (FOP) Daryna Khripkova ("we", "the Studio"), Ukraine. The Princess Dance Studio app serves the studio's clients: viewing schedules, booking classes, camps and events, purchasing subscriptions, and paying for services.

Privacy contact: arishkaaa393@gmail.com.

DataPurpose

We do not collect data for advertising, do not track you across other apps, and never sell your data.

3. Data processors

  • Supabase — secure data storage and authentication;
  • TurboSMS — delivery of SMS sign-in codes;
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery;
  • LiqPay (JSC CB "PrivatBank") — card payment processing.

4. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data (name, phone, photo) is erased, and payment records are anonymized — they are no longer linked to you and are kept solely for the Studio's financial records.

5. Security

Data is transmitted only over secure channels (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is segregated: clients see only their own records, trainers see their groups' bookings, and full access is limited to the Studio's administration.

6. Your rights

  • view and correct your data directly in the app;
  • delete your account: in the app via Settings → Delete account, or through the deletion request page;
  • contact us about your data: arishkaaa393@gmail.com;
  • withdraw consent by stopping use of the app and deleting your account.

7. Children

Accounts are created by adults — students' parents or adult clients of the Studio. The app is not intended for independent use by children and does not collect children's data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address; the effective date is shown at the top.
